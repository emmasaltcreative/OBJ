Shipping Policy

Oh Betty Jaletti
Last updated: November 7, 2026

At Oh Betty Jaletti, we believe the details matter — from the pieces we select to the way they arrive at your door. Every order is prepared with care, whether it is an everyday favorite or a piece made especially for you.

How Long It Takes

Two things make up the wait: the days we spend packing your parcel by hand at the house, and the days it spends with the carrier. Both are listed here, along with the total.

  • Standard — arrives in 5–10 business days. Packed in 3–5 business days, then 2–5 business days in the post. $8.00, or free on orders of $70.00 or more.
  • Express — arrives in 4–8 business days. Packed in 3–5 business days, then 1–3 business days in the post. $15.00. Express buys a faster carrier, not a faster packing bench.
  • The Scented Letter, our free sample — allow 1–2 weeks. Sent by ordinary post, without tracking.

Business days exclude weekends and federal holidays. An order placed on a Friday evening begins its count on Monday.

Order Processing

In-stock orders generally ship within 3–5 business days, excluding weekends and federal holidays. Processing time is separate from carrier transit time.

During collection launches or periods of high demand, processing may take longer. If your order will take materially longer than the window above, we will write to you with a revised date, and you may cancel for a full refund.

Shipping Rates & Delivery

We currently offer shipping to eligible addresses within the United States.

Shipping methods, their costs, and the estimated arrival windows above are shown again at checkout before you pay. Complimentary shipping may be offered on select products or promotions.

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking information when available.

Preorders & Small-Batch Releases

Some Oh Betty Jaletti pieces are available for preorder or produced in limited quantities.

Preorder items will display an estimated shipping date or fulfillment window before purchase. Payment is collected according to the terms disclosed at checkout.

If a preorder experiences a delay beyond the promised shipping timeframe, we will contact you with updated information and the option to accept the delay or cancel for a full refund, as required by applicable law. You may also cancel a preorder at any time before it ships, for a full refund, simply by writing to us.

Orders Containing Multiple Items

Orders containing both in-stock and preorder items may ship together once all items are available. If you would like the in-stock piece sooner, place it as a separate order. Where separate shipments are offered, any additional shipping charges will be disclosed before purchase.

Fragrance Shipping

Due to transportation regulations, alcohol-based fragrances are shipped through eligible ground services only. Fragrance orders are not available for international shipping or delivery to APO, FPO, or DPO addresses. Other destination restrictions may apply.

Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Packages

We take care to package your purchases securely.

If your order arrives damaged, appears lost, or has a delivery issue, please contact us at hello@ohbettyjaletti.com with your order number and a brief description. Photos of damaged items or packaging are helpful when available.

We will work with you and the carrier to investigate and arrange an appropriate resolution consistent with applicable consumer protections.

Questions?

For help with an order, please contact hello@ohbettyjaletti.com or visit our website at ohbettyjaletti.com.
